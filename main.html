Model assumptions

  • Per-pass reactor conversion is an illustrative correlation fitted to typical published trends for N/C ratio, H₂O/CO₂ ratio and temperature — it is not licensed kinetics data from any process technology provider.
  • The recirculation section is lumped into a single recycle stream rather than modelled as sequential HP/LP decomposition stages.
  • No rigorous VLE or activity-coefficient thermodynamic package is used; stream temperatures/pressures reflect typical industrial values for a CO₂-stripping train.
  • Steady-state, single-train basis; start-up, shutdown and off-spec transients are excluded.
  • Utility consumption (steam, power, cooling water) is scaled from order-of-magnitude industry benchmarks per tonne of urea, not equipment-specific duty calculations.
  • Equipment sizing (reactor volume) is order-of-magnitude only, from residence time and an assumed loop-fluid density.
